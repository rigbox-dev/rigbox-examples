A local service with a Rigbox URL.

This page comes from the Python process you started locally. Keep your tunnel command running while you use its HTTPS URL.

Check the local service

A small service you can inspect

The demo serves this page, its stylesheet, a health check, and a bounded POST /echo response. It does not provide file browsing or execute commands.

Private access is the default. Changing tunnel access changes who can reach the whole local service.
